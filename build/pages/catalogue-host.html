Host a program for your members.

You convene an audience, a chamber, an association, a public body, and would receive a funded program at no cost to yourself.

The office will ask who your members are, what you have hosted before, whether your institution places restrictions on underwriting or on sponsors, what approvals hosting needs on your side, and what your calendar and your capability allow.
