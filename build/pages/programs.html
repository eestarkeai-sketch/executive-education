Programs

One library. Every format. Every room.

A Program is a body of intellectual work that exists once. It is delivered through a format, a keynote, a working session, a series, an intensive, under one of three commercial arrangements, and sometimes through a specialized product. It is never duplicated because it is delivered to a university rather than a company.

How the library is organized

Program

The canonical work: its identity, who it is for, what it prepares, what it requires, and the evidence behind it.

Format

How it is delivered on the day. Executive Keynote, Executive Conversation, Roundtable, Working Session, Leadership Laboratory, Series, Intensive, Advisory.

Commercial arrangement

Whether an organization is buying it for its own people, an underwriter is funding it for a community, or an institution is engaging it academically.

Delivery product

Where a program is delivered through Starke Foundry or The Starke Experience, that product carries its own operating logic.
