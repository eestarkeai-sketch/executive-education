Academic Engagements

Institution first. The relationship before the engagement.

Work with universities, business schools, accelerators and academic programs is shaped by the institution: its approval structures, its calendar, its students and founders, and the line between them and the executives Ethan Starke otherwise serves. It begins as a relationship and grows into engagements.
